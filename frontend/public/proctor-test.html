How to trigger each check

Every check below counts as 1 warning. It takes 6 to end the test — a single glance away or a momentary drop-out won't kick you.

📱 Hold a phone up to the camera → phone_detected
🙈 Step out of frame for ~1s → no_face
👥 Get a second person in frame → multiple_faces
👀 Turn your head fully left/right and hold it → looking_away (mapped to no_face on the backend)
🪟 Switch tabs / minimize → tab_switch
⛶ Click "Enter fullscreen" then press Esc → fullscreen_exit
